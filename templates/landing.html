How it works

Add what's due

Course, title, due date and a difficulty from 1 to 5. It takes about ten seconds per assignment.

See the ranking

Every pending assignment gets a priority score. The highest one is pinned to the top of your dashboard.

Start with the top one

Mark it done and the next one moves up. Search and filter by course, status or due date any time.

One simple score, no black box

score = urgency + difficulty × 2

Urgency is 10 minus the days left (never below 0), and 15 once something is overdue. A difficulty-4 assignment due in 2 days scores 8 + 8 = 16.
